Some fun facts about me:

Born and raised in NYC. I graduated from American University in Washington, D.C. with a degree in Political Communications. I have reported on COVID-19, national politics and some election reporting. I am really excited to report on City Hall and other New York City centered news. My favorite color is pink.

Some of my favorite recent news stories:

  1. directions_runThe Last Lucille Roberts: A profile on the life of the founder of the iconic women's gym, now down to their last location in Forest Hills. This story was so fun and unexpected. I was happy to learn about the real woman behind Lucille Roberts, an immigrant who was able to build her own empire.
  2. poll Mamdani declares victory in NYC's Democratic mayoral primary as Cuomo concedes: What a great story for a great night. For this one, the news itself put this story in my top three. I did not think I would ever see someone like me, a South-Asian Muslim, with beliefs that reflect mine; a guy that went to high school with my sister, would get nominated to lead NYC. Getting to see Cuomo concede was just an added benefit.
  3. access_alarms Twelve Minutes and a Life: This piece was absolutely haunting. Nobody's life can be summarized in a few thousand words but after reading this I did feel as if I was able to see Ahmed Arbaury through the lens of those close to him.
